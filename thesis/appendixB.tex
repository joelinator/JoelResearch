\section{Empirical Training and Validation Trajectories}

Figure~\ref{fig:training_validation_curves} illustrates the optimization and generative validation dynamics of DFlowNovo across the complete 30-epoch training schedule on the Nine-Species biological benchmark.

\begin{figure}[!ht]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{images/training_validation_curves.png}
\caption{Empirical training and validation trajectories of DFlowNovo over 30 epochs. (a) Total loss convergence showing raw mini-batch training loss, smoothed training trend, and validation loss. (b) Decomposed training and validation trajectories for the discrete flow matching cross-entropy loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{seq}}$) and the auxiliary length classification loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{len}}$). (c) Validation generation metrics across epochs, showing strict exact sequence accuracy, isobaric Leucine/Isoleucine accuracy, and length prediction accuracy. (d) Residue-level amino acid F1 score progression alongside the AdamW cosine learning rate decay schedule.}
\label{fig:training_validation_curves}
\end{figure}

The empirical trajectories highlight several distinct dynamical regimes:
\begin{enumerate}[(i)]
\item \textbf{Rapid Flow Initialization:} During the initial five epochs, both the training loss and validation loss decrease sharply from $1.11$ to below $0.46$. This initial descent corresponds to the network learning the dominant marginal distributions of tryptic peptide fragments and establishing reliable spectral-to-sequence cross-attention alignments.
\item \textbf{Component Loss Decoupling:} As shown in Figure~\ref{fig:training_validation_curves}(b), the length classification loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{len}}$) drops swiftly within the first two epochs and stabilizes around $0.20$, achieving over $81.4\%$ exact sequence length prediction accuracy on held-out spectra. Meanwhile, the sequence flow matching loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{seq}}$) continues its steady descent, driving incremental refinements in residue boundary delineation.
\item \textbf{Generative Validation Plateau:} Unlike autoregressive decoders that often suffer from exposure bias and early validation divergence, DFlowNovo's reverse flow sampling accuracy (evaluated every epoch) rises monotonically through epoch 10 and maintains a stable plateau from epoch 15 through 30. Peak validation exact match reaches $47.13\%$ (strict) and $57.12\%$ (isobaric I/L), with residue-level F1 stabilizing at $73.45\%$.
\item \textbf{Absence of Degradation:} The smooth cosine decay of the learning rate (Figure~\ref{fig:training_validation_curves}(d)) from $5.0 \times 10^{-4}$ down to $1.0 \times 10^{-6}$ provides stable late-stage regularization, preventing gradient oscillations or loss degradation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Empirical CTMC Flow Dynamics and Schedule Analysis}

Figure~\ref{fig:ctmc_flow_dynamics_app} provides empirical validation of Continuous-Time Markov Chain (CTMC) flow dynamics evaluated across 10,000 biological spectra from the Nine-Species benchmark.

\begin{figure}[!ht]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.88\textwidth]{images/ctmc_flow_dynamics.png}
\caption{Empirical verification of Continuous-Time Markov Chain (CTMC) flow dynamics evaluated across 10,000 biological spectra. (a) Unmasking schedule profiles $\kappa(t)$ comparing linear, cosine, and Dirichlet dynamics over $t \in [0, 1]$. (b) Positional categorical Shannon entropy decay across reverse flow sampling steps, showing progressive uncertainty reduction. (c) Token commitment velocity $\frac{\mathrm{d}\kappa}{\mathrm{d}t}$ across integration time. (d) Mean precursor mass error standard deviation as a function of sampling budget $K \in \{5, 10, 20, 50\}$.}
\label{fig:ctmc_flow_dynamics_app}
\end{figure}

The empirical trajectories demonstrate the core advantages of the cosine schedule in tandem mass spectrometry decoding:
\begin{enumerate}[(i)]
\item \textbf{Controlled Information Integration:} As evidenced in Figure~\ref{fig:ctmc_flow_dynamics_app}(a) and (c), the cosine schedule enforces $\kappa'(0) = 0$, ensuring zero initial unmasking velocity. During early sampling steps ($t \in [0, 0.2]$), the bidirectional decoder processes the complete set of spectral peaks and precursor conditioning tokens without making premature irreversible amino acid assignments.
\item \textbf{Monotonic Entropy Reduction:} As illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:ctmc_flow_dynamics_app}(b), positional categorical Shannon entropy decreases monotonically and smoothly across the 20 Euler integration steps. In contrast to linear unmasking which causes abrupt late-stage collapse, the cosine schedule distributes residue commitments over the middle integration interval ($t \in [0.3, 0.8]$).
\item \textbf{Precursor Mass Error Convergence:} Figure~\ref{fig:ctmc_flow_dynamics_app}(d) demonstrates that integration budgets of $K \ge 20$ achieve asymptotic convergence in precursor mass accuracy, validating the choice of $K=20$ as the production operational budget.
\end{enumerate}
